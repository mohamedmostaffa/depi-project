## 2. Document Extraction Summaries

### D1: Brand Identity & Official Policies Manual (7 Pages)
- **Section 01 (p. 2) — Brand Identity & Core Positioning:**
  - Label Description: Contemporary streetwear and apparel label built around bold visual culture, iconic sports and pop-culture references, and everyday oversized silhouettes.
  - Product Architecture:
    1. Graphic Crewnecks & Hoodies: Heavyweight relaxed-fit tops crafted for cold-weather layering, 100% cotton hoodies, high-contrast crewnecks.
    2. Sports & Vintage-Inspired Tops: Football jerseys, half-zip pullovers, retro-styled track jackets.
    3. Bottoms & Sets: Washed wide-leg denim, relaxed sweatpants, coordinated monochrome sets.
  - Core Brand Pillars: Cultural Relevance, Unapologetic Visual Impact, Everyday Quality, Transparent Service.
- **Section 02 (pp. 2–3) — Logo & Visual Guidelines:**
  - Primary Mark: Crimson bubble typography framed by an oval ring and centered 5-point star.
  - Official Palette:
    - RLVNT Crimson: `#8F1D1D`
    - Streetwear Black: `#111111`
    - Poster Off-White: `#F8F6F4`
    - Crisp White: `#FFFFFF`
- **Section 03 (pp. 3–4) — Ordering, Pricing & Payment:**
  - Official channels: Website and official social channels.
  - Order verification: High-value or COD orders verified via phone/WhatsApp prior to dispatch.
- **Section 04 (p. 4) — Shipping & Delivery:**
  - Protective garment packaging to prevent transit damage, moisture, or print scuffing.
  - Inspection upon delivery: Customers must inspect packages upon arrival and report transit damage within reporting window with photos.
- **Section 05 (p. 5) — Returns, Exchanges & Refund Policy:**
  - Window: Return/exchange requests must be submitted within standard return window from delivery date.
  - Condition: Unworn, unwashed, free of perfume, stains, or pet hair, original packaging with all tags attached.
  - Size/Style Exchange: Swap for another available size or equal-value item. Customer covers exchange return shipping.
  - Defective/Wrong Item: 100% covered by RLVNT (priority replacement or full refund after photo verification).
  - Standard Return: Refund to original payment method or store credit after warehouse inspection. Shipping is non-refundable.
  - Non-Returnable Items: Final-sale clearance items, washed/altered garments, items missing tags. (Confirming: ZERO cosmetic/hygiene exclusions).
- **Section 06 (pp. 5–6) — Sizing & Garment Care:**
  - Fit: Relaxed streetwear proportions / oversized fits. Review size chart.
  - Washing: Inside-out, machine wash cold (30°C max), gentle cycle with similar colors.
  - Detergent: Mild liquid detergent only. No chlorine bleach, harsh stain removers, or fabric softeners on prints.
  - Drying & Ironing: Hang dry or lay flat in shade (no high-heat tumble dry); steam lightly or iron inside-out on low heat (never iron directly on artwork).
  - Exclusions: Damage from improper washing/drying/ironing is not covered as a manufacturing defect.
- **Section 07 (p. 6) — Privacy & IP Protection:**
  - Customer data used strictly for fulfillment and support; no resale of lists.
  - Brand assets (crimson wordmark, star logo, lookbooks) strictly protected.
- **Section 08 (p. 7) — Customer Support:**
  - Channels for support inquiries and updates.
